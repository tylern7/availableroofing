Questions

A few things people ask

How do I know if I need a repair or a replacement?

If the roof has frequent leaks, visible wear, sagging sections or insulation problems, it may need replacement. We inspect first and recommend the most cost-effective option.

Which flat roofing system is a good fit?

TPO and EPDM are popular for durability and energy performance. Torch-down is a strong waterproofing option. We select the system based on the building, the slope and the climate.

Do you work on both homes and businesses?

Yes. We provide commercial and residential roofing in Oakville and the GTA.

How are estimates handled?

Costs depend on roof size, material and damage. We provide free estimates with straightforward pricing.
